Bugfix: Messages and options in the account wizard are fully readable

The pages of the account wizard scroll when their content does not fit,
so long messages, longer translations or larger fonts are no longer cut
off below the buttons. The message about the missing "OAuth2" app is one
short paragraph for users and administrators. The options on the
"Advanced" page wrap instead of being cut off at "(saves hard drive
space)", and the logo of the address page comes back after a message.
